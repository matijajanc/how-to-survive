# Quick-Reference Cards

*The numbers you need at 3 a.m., by torchlight, without reading a chapter.*

**PRINT THESE FIRST.** Print two sets, laminate them or use clear plastic
wallets, and **put them on the wall** — not in the binder. One set in the
kitchen, one in the emergency kit. Cards 9 and 10 have blanks: **fill them in
by hand, in pen, today.**

Suggested posting: cards 1–3 by the water store · card 4 by every stove and
heater · card 5 in the medical kit · cards 6–8 in the kitchen ·
cards 9–10 by the front door.

---

## CARD 1 — MAKING WATER SAFE

```
ALWAYS DO BOTH:   FILTER (removes dirt, bacteria, protozoa)
                  THEN DISINFECT (kills viruses)

CLOUDY WATER FIRST: settle 2–12 h · pour through 4–8 layers of cotton
                    · a pinch of alum makes it clear in 30 min

BOILING            Rolling boil 1 MINUTE     (3 minutes above 2,000 m)
                   Kills everything. Costs ~1 kg of wood per 10–15 L.

BLEACH             Unscented 5–6% sodium hypochlorite, no additives
  clear water      2 DROPS per LITRE        wait 30 min
  cloudy or cold   4 DROPS per LITRE        wait 60 min
  40 L container   10 mL  (2 teaspoons)
  200 L drum       50 mL
  >> You should smell faint chlorine afterwards. If not, repeat and wait again.
  >> Bleach loses ~20% strength a year. Date the bottle. Replace annually.

NaDCC TABLETS      One tablet per the packet dose. Wait 30 min.
                   5–10 year shelf life. The best thing to store.

POOL SHOCK         Calcium hypochlorite 65–70%, PLAIN (no algaecide/stabiliser)
  make stock       7 g (1 heaped tsp) into 7.5 L water
  then dose        1 part stock to 100 parts water · wait 30 min
  >> Store OUTSIDE the house, dry, sealed, away from fuel and acids.

NEVER DRINK        heating-system water · pool water · anything with
                   antifreeze, inhibitor or algaecide
KEEP SEPARATE      Mark containers RAW and DRINK. Never swap them.
```

**HOW MUCH:** 15–25 L per person per day sustainable · **5–7 L survival minimum**
· chicken 0.5 L · goat 8 L · dog 2 L

## CARD 2 — DEHYDRATION & ORAL REHYDRATION

```
★ DIARRHOEA AND VOMITING KILL BY DEHYDRATION — FAST.
★ Children and the elderly go downhill in hours, not days.

ORAL REHYDRATION SOLUTION (ORS) — make it exactly:

     1 LITRE clean water
   + 6 LEVEL teaspoons SUGAR
   + 1/2 LEVEL teaspoon SALT
     stir until dissolved

  >> Too much salt is dangerous. Level spoons. It should taste
     no saltier than tears.
  >> Sip continuously — small amounts, often. Keep sipping even if vomiting.
  >> Adult: 200–400 mL after every loose stool.
     Child: 50–100 mL after every loose stool.
  >> Keep breastfeeding. Keep eating if they can.

SHOP SACHETS ARE BETTER. Keep 40+. They weigh nothing and cost almost nothing.

SIGNS OF SERIOUS DEHYDRATION — SEEK HELP (see CARD 5)
  no urine for 8–12 hours · very dark urine · sunken eyes · no tears in a child
  dry mouth · dizzy on standing · skin stays tented when pinched
  drowsy, floppy, confused · fast breathing or fast weak pulse

DO NOT give anti-diarrhoeal drugs if there is BLOOD or a HIGH FEVER.
Rehydration is the treatment. Fluids, fluids, fluids.
```

## CARD 3 — BLEACH & DISINFECTION

```
Household bleach = 5–6% sodium hypochlorite. Mix FRESH — it loses
strength within about 24 hours once diluted.

USE                                 DILUTION            CONTACT TIME
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Drinking water                      2 drops per litre   30 min
Surfaces, general cleaning          1:100  (10 mL/L)     1 min
Body fluids, vomit, faeces          1:10   (100 mL/L)   10 min
Sick-room surfaces                  1:10                10 min
Dishes/cloths from a sick person    1:100               10 min
Boot dip / threshold footbath       1:50                 -

★ CLEAN FIRST, THEN DISINFECT. Disinfectant on a dirty surface does little.
★ NEVER MIX BLEACH WITH: ammonia · acids · toilet cleaner · vinegar
  It releases chlorine gas. In a small room this will seriously hurt you.
★ Ventilate while using it. Gloves on.

ALTERNATIVES   70% alcohol (fast, flammable) · hydrogen peroxide 3%
               boiling water (dishes, cloths) · soap + scrubbing (always first)
NO SUPPLY      wood ash (alkaline, for latrines) · garden lime (pits, drums)

HANDWASHING BEATS ALL OF IT.
Soap + 20 seconds + rinse. After the toilet, after waste, after animals,
after outside, before food, before eating, before and after caring for the ill.
```

## CARD 4 — CARBON MONOXIDE — THIS IS THE ONE THAT KILLS

```
  ██  CO IS COLOURLESS, ODOURLESS AND TASTELESS.  ██
  ██  IT KILLS IN MINUTES. THE EARLY SYMPTOMS     ██
  ██  STOP YOU REALISING YOU NEED TO ACT.         ██

NEVER, EVER, INDOORS — not in a garage, cellar, porch, tent or caravan,
not "with a window open", not "just for a bit":

    ✗ CHARCOAL or a BBQ          ✗ GENERATOR
    ✗ ANY PETROL ENGINE          ✗ PATIO / FLUELESS HEATER
    ✗ A GAS COOKER USED AS A ROOM HEATER
    ✗ ANY FLAME OR STOVE INSIDE A TENT OR BED CANOPY

GENERATOR: outdoors only · at least 5 m from any door, window or vent ·
           exhaust pointing AWAY · never refuel it hot

ANY FLAME uses oxygen and makes CO2, water vapour and some CO.
VENTILATE — a 2–5 cm window gap costs a little heat and buys a lot of safety.
NEVER sleep with a flueless combustion appliance running.

SYMPTOMS   headache · nausea · dizziness · sleepiness · confusion ·
           weakness · everyone in the room feels ill at once ·
           BETTER WHEN OUTSIDE, WORSE WHEN INSIDE

IF SUSPECTED:  1. GET EVERYONE OUT, NOW.
               2. Open everything. Turn the appliance off if you safely can.
               3. DO NOT GO BACK IN.
               4. Seek medical help — CO poisoning needs treatment.

FIT CO ALARMS (EN 50291): every sleeping room, every room with a
combustion appliance, the wall nearest the generator. €20 each.
TEST THEM MONTHLY. REPLACE EVERY 5–7 YEARS.
```

## CARD 5 — SEEK MEDICAL HELP NOW

```
An overwhelmed hospital is still vastly better than a kitchen table.
Isolation is not worth a life. GO, or call, for any of these:

BREATHING   SpO2 below 92% (or a clear fall from their normal)
            severe breathlessness · too breathless to finish a sentence
            blue or grey lips or face · coughing blood
            chest pain or pressure

BRAIN       confusion · cannot be roused · unresponsive · seizure
            severe headache with a stiff neck or light hurting the eyes

RASH        a fever with a rash that does NOT fade when you press a
            glass against it  >> possible meningitis/sepsis — EMERGENCY

FLUIDS      no urine for 8–12 hours · sunken eyes · no tears in a child
            skin stays tented when pinched · dizzy or fainting on standing

OTHER       any fever in a baby under 3 months
            severe abdominal pain, especially lower right (appendicitis)
            bleeding you cannot stop · a suspected fracture or head injury
            a burn larger than the person's palm, or on face/hands/genitals
            anything that frightens you AND is getting worse

TWICE A DAY FOR ANYONE ILL, WRITE IT DOWN:
  time · temperature · pulse · breaths/min · SpO2 · fluids in · urine out
  · symptoms · medicine given
A written trend tells you what a single reading cannot — and it is exactly
what a professional will ask for.
```

## CARD 6 — THE TRIGGER LADDER

```
GREEN   Normal. Build to your target level. Rotate stock. Train. Grow.
        Check outbreak news monthly. Nothing is urgent.

AMBER   >> Sustained human-to-human spread OUTSIDE the origin country. <<
        ★ THIS IS THE TRIGGER. NOT "cases in my country" — that is too late.
        ACT WITHIN 72 HOURS — see CARD 7. This is the single most valuable
        action in the whole plan and the window is only a few days wide.

ORANGE  Community transmission confirmed in your country.
        Withdraw from optional contact · work from home · quarantine
        incoming goods and people · final purchases BY DELIVERY ·
        start sprouts and microgreens · set up the isolation room and PPE
        BEFORE anyone is ill · switch to the crisis daily routine now.

RED     Hospitals saturated, OR utilities visibly failing, OR spread
        in your street.
        Full isolation · stored/produced resources only · war-footing
        hygiene · daily health check for everyone · light, noise and
        smell discipline · daily radio check-in with the group ·
        ★ START RATIONING ON DAY ONE, not when stocks look low.
          Set the daily allowance for a 12-MONTH horizon. It is far
          easier to relax a ration than to impose one late.

BLACK   Utilities down indefinitely, no authority response.
        Full off-grid. Treated water only. Humanure/latrine. Warm room
        only. Cooking on wood. Watch by rotation with the group.
        Preserve everything perishable immediately.

COMING BACK DOWN: step down ONE level after TWO WEEKS of improvement.
Restock to target BEFORE relaxing the ration. Do not live in RED for years.
```

## CARD 7 — AMBER: THE 72-HOUR LIST

```
You have about 72 hours before this becomes impossible and expensive.
One trip, one delivery round, one list.

[ ] FILL every water container — bath, bladder, cans, pots, bottles,
    the washing machine drum
[ ] FILL the car's fuel tank and every fuel can
[ ] REFILL every prescription; ask for an early refill and a written repeat
[ ] DRAW CASH, in small notes
[ ] TOP UP: flour · oil · rice · pasta · beans · sugar · salt · coffee ·
    tinned fish · UHT milk · BABY FORMULA · PET FOOD
[ ] TOP UP: soap · bleach · toilet paper · menstrual supplies · nappies ·
    batteries · matches
[ ] TOP UP: paracetamol · ibuprofen · ORS · antihistamine · vitamins
[ ] ORDER the long-lead items you postponed — filters, seed, spares, a
    battery, gas cylinders, animal feed
    >> Delivery times collapse immediately after this point.
[ ] EXTRA GAS CYLINDERS / PROPANE
[ ] 6 MONTHS of animal feed
[ ] TELL the mutual-aid group. Agree a check-in schedule.
[ ] START masking in crowded indoor places; cut optional exposure.
    The goal is to be UNINFECTED AND STOCKED when the wave arrives.
[ ] SWITCH to the crisis daily routine so it is habit before it matters.
```

## CARD 8 — THE DAILY ROUTINE

```
07:00  Wake — everyone, same time. Drifting sleep is the first crack.
       Health check: anyone symptomatic? Temp + SpO2 if so. Log it.
       Light the stove. Kettle and the big pot on.
       WATER: check levels, run the filter, fill the handwash station.
       POWER: check battery %, clear the panels, plan the day's loads.
       ANIMALS: feed, water (break ice), collect eggs, check security.
       Rinse the sprout jars.
08:00  BREAKFAST TOGETHER.
09:00  Radio check-in with the group.
       Main work block — assigned the night before. Everyone has a job.
12:30  Lunch.
13:30  Second work block. Children: school work.
15:00  START THE EVENING MEAL — get it boiling, then into the thermal
       cooker so it finishes itself with no more fuel.
       Finish every daylight task before dusk. Always.
17:00  SECURE FOR NIGHT: animals in, doors locked, tools in, blackout
       curtains closed, water containers refilled.
       Rinse the sprout jars.
18:00  DINNER TOGETHER. No radio. No crisis talk.
19:00  Radio check-in. NEWS: 20 minutes, ONE source, then off.
       Household meeting, 10 min: today, tomorrow, who does what,
       is anyone struggling.
20:00  NON-PRODUCTIVE TIME, DELIBERATELY. Cards, music, reading aloud.
       This is the maintenance schedule for the people. It is not optional.
22:00  Lights out. Log the day: weather, stores used, work done, changes.

★ ROTATE THE WORST JOBS ON A WRITTEN ROTA. Nothing corrodes a household
  faster than one person silently emptying the toilet bucket every day.
★ ONE FULL REST DAY A WEEK. Defend it. Burnout is a survival risk.
```

## CARD 11 — RATION & CALORIE MATHS

```
DAILY NEED       Working adult      2,800 – 3,000 kcal
                 Sedentary adult    1,900 – 2,400 kcal
                 Child 4–10         1,400 – 1,900 kcal
                 Teenager           2,200 – 3,600 kcal
                 Pregnant +300 · Breastfeeding +500

★ Crisis life IS manual work, and a cold house burns hundreds more.
  Undereating destroys the physical work you must do AND your immune
  system, exactly when infection is the threat.

ROUGH kcal PER KG (dry staples average ~3,400 kcal/kg)
  Oil 8,800 · Sugar 4,000 · Milk powder 4,900 · Pasta 3,600 · Rice 3,600
  Oats 3,800 · Wheat 3,400 · Beans 3,400 · Honey 3,040 · Potato 770

ENDURANCE CHECK — do this MONTHLY and write the number down:
     total kcal in store  ÷  household kcal per day  =  DAYS REMAINING

ONE YEAR, ONE ADULT:  grain 180 kg · legumes 30 kg · fat 12 kg (13 L)
                      sugar 20 kg · dairy powder 8 kg · SALT 4 kg
A FAMILY OF FOUR, ONE YEAR: about 965 kg ≈ 1.5–2 m3 ≈ €900–1,600

★ VITAMIN C: scurvy in 8–12 weeks on stored food alone.
  SPROUTS (3–5 days, no light, no power) · sauerkraut · vitamin C tablets.
  5 kg of sprouting seed per person costs €25 and keeps 4 years.
  It is the best-value item in the entire plan.
```
