# Worksheet 2 — Shopping Lists

**Buy in this order.** Every list assumes the one before it is complete.
Quantities are for **a family of four (2 adults, 2 children)** — scale by
`your people ÷ 4`. Prices are indicative EUR and will vary.

🏡 = land/house track · 🏢 = apartment track · ⬛ = both

> **The rule that overrides everything on this page:** buy for whichever
> category has the **lowest endurance in days** on your audit. A cheap item in
> your weakest category beats an expensive item in your strongest one, always.

## What to buy the moment you go to 🟡 AMBER

*You have 72 hours before this becomes impossible. One trip, one delivery round,
one list. Everything here is a top-up of things you should already have.*

```
[ ] Fill EVERY water container, the bath, the bladder, pots, the washing machine
[ ] Fill the car's fuel tank and every fuel can
[ ] Refill/request early every prescription; get a written repeat
[ ] Draw out cash, in small notes
[ ] Top up: flour, oil, rice, pasta, beans, sugar, salt, coffee, tinned fish,
    UHT milk, formula/baby food, pet food
[ ] Top up: soap, bleach, toilet paper, menstrual supplies, nappies, batteries
[ ] Top up: paracetamol, ibuprofen, ORS, antihistamine, vitamins
[ ] Order the long-lead items you postponed: filters, seed, spares, a battery,
    gas cylinders, feed  ← delivery times collapse after this point
[ ] Buy the extra gas cylinders / propane now
[ ] Order animal feed for 6 months
[ ] Tell the mutual-aid group; agree a check-in schedule
[ ] Start masking in crowded indoor spaces and cut optional exposure
[ ] Move to the crisis daily routine so it is habit before it matters
```
